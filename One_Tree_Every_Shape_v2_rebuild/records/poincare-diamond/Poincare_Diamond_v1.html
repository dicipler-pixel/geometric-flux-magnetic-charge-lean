page map (edge, Poincaré radius tanh(ρ/2))
physical map = direction diagonal: B ⊖ A = Γ(v/u) (Klein / admittance tanh ρ)
size diagonal A ⊕ B = Γ(uv)
(1−AB)/(1+AB): equals cosh ρ on the cross diagonal (field share is its inverse)

Cable segments (with loss and phase too) move the point straight, one peel step per edge, in any order. A junction between two different lines mixes the books: the point jumps off its line, and the order of pieces starts to matter. That is where the diamond stops and the ball is needed.
